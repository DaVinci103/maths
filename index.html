Maths

The section about elementary set theory explains sets as well as certain set constructions (such as sums, products and the set of natural numbers). In this section, the notion of a function is taken as a primitive concept, and everything else is build on that.

The section about cardinals and ordinals explains cardinals, ordinals and how to do arithmetic with them. In this section, we explore many equivalences of the axiom of choice, such as the well-ordering principle, cardinal trichotomy and Zorn's lemma.

The section Algebra is about abstract algebra. The first chapter gives an overview of the algebraic structures we'll explore and the second chapter explains what a category is. After that, we'll explore the different algebraic structures in the order: groups, rings, modules, fields. No prerequisists are needed for this section. This section is not fully planned out yet.
